    Info

    Before you book.

    What's the difference between the spaces?

    The ground floor splits into two sides: the Bar Side with the stone bar and café seating, and the Stage Side with the stage riser, booths and garage doors. Book one side, both sides together, or the Upstairs for major parties and events.

    How do I book?

    Send a request from the Book tab. We reply within 24 hours to confirm your date and details. Nothing is charged when you send a request.

    Can I bring my own caterer, DJ or decorator?

    Yes. Food vendors must be licensed and insured, and every vendor needs venue approval ahead of time.

    Can I tour the space first?

    Yes. Mention it in your request or call us and we'll set up a time.

    What's included?

    Tables and chairs, house lighting and private use of the space you book.

    Responsibility & damages

    Hosts and promoters are responsible for their guests and liable for any damage to the property, equipment or facility during the rental.

    Security

    Professional security is required for most events. All security must be provided or approved by Warehouse West management before the event.

    Food & catering

    All food and catering vendors must be licensed, insured and approved by the venue.

    Prohibited

    No unapproved pyrotechnics (including cold sparks), fog machines, open flames or illegal substances. Violations end the event immediately without a refund.

    Insurance

    Some events may need a Certificate of Insurance naming Warehouse West Event Collective as an additional insured.
